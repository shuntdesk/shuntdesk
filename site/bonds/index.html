Progress is the ETH a curve has raised against the ETH it needs to bond, read from the curve contract itself. The pace is the change over the last hour, and "at this pace" only carries that pace on; curves stall and fall back far more often than they finish. Launches that raised under 1% in their first six hours are dropped from the board. Not advice.
