Using the site

What this is

Shuntdesk reads public data about tokens on Robinhood Chain, runs fixed checks on it, and shows a verdict. It also runs paper trading desks and a game: simulated accounts with simulated money, priced with real market data. Every balance, position, fee, leverage figure and liquidation on the site is simulated. Nothing is bought, sold, borrowed or lent.

Not advice

Nothing on the site, the bot, the API or our posts is financial, investment, legal or tax advice, or an offer or invitation to trade anything. A clean verdict is not a recommendation to buy. A danger verdict is not an accusation of fraud against anyone. Text written by a language model can be mistaken.

How far to trust it

The checks see only what is public and can miss what is not. The data comes from other services, which can be late, incomplete or wrong. Paper results say nothing about what real trading would return: real markets have costs, delays and risks the simulation does not model, and leveraged trading can lose more than a simulation suggests. Use what you find here at your own risk.
