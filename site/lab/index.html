Each new token on Robinhood Chain with 25 or more holders is logged 30 to 90 minutes after launch, scanned by the radar, and priced again an hour and a day later. "Lost half" is a fall of 50% or more; a token with no price later counts as lost. Lift is how many times more often the tokens a rule catches lost half than the ones it does not; it shows once both sides have 20 tokens or more. A lift near 1 means the rule tells you little. A token nobody trades keeps its last price, so thin pools rarely show a fall: a low lift on a liquidity or holder rule can mean quiet, not safe. Past results, not a forecast.
