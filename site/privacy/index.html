In your browser

What stays with you

Your paper desk and its history, your recent tickets, your watchlist, the handle you choose, and your game save in The Yard (progress, cash, missions, settings) are kept in your browser's own storage. They are not tied to your identity. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Services your browser talks to directly

To read prices and chain data, your browser contacts DexScreener, GeckoTerminal, Blockscout, GoPlus, Binance and public Robinhood Chain nodes. Each of them sees your IP address and what you asked for, under its own privacy policy. The site's security policy blocks any other outside connection.

On our server

Tickets

When you run a ticket: the token's address, the verdict, the score and the short result of each check. These calls are public: they appear on the site and in the open dataset on Hugging Face. They carry nothing about you.

Boards

When you post to the board of desks: your handle and your paper equity, trade count and reset count. When you post a shift in The Yard: your handle, which trains you got and which lever you pulled. Handles are shown publicly; pick one that does not identify you.

Phone alerts

If you turn on alerts for your watchlist, the server keeps the push address your browser gives it and the tokens you watch, so it can send the alerts. Blocking notifications for the site in your browser stops them. Old push addresses are dropped as new ones come in.

Abuse limits

To limit abuse, the server keeps your IP address next to a count of recent requests for up to an hour, then drops it. Cloudflare, which hosts the site, processes requests under its own privacy policy.

Language models

The conductor

A question to the conductor, with the findings it refers to, is sent to a language-model provider to be answered (through Surplus, with OpenRouter as the fallback). Do not type personal information into that box.

The second read

For Pons tokens, only the token's public figures are sent to ChargeAI on Surplus.

Telegram

The bot

@shuntdesk_bot receives the messages sent to it, and in groups it answers only commands, mentions and replies to it. A question you ask it is sent to the language model to be answered. Telegram's own privacy policy covers everything else in the app.
