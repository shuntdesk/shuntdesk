Using the site

What this is

Shuntdesk reads public data about tokens on Robinhood Chain, runs fixed checks on it, and shows a verdict. It also runs paper trading desks: simulated accounts with simulated money, priced with real market data. Every balance, position, fee, leverage figure and liquidation on the site is simulated. Nothing is bought, sold, borrowed or lent.

Not advice

Nothing on the site is financial, investment, legal or tax advice, or an offer or invitation to trade anything. A clean verdict is not a recommendation to buy. A danger verdict is not an accusation of fraud against anyone. The conductor's text is written by a language model from the findings on the page and can be mistaken.

How far to trust it

The checks see only what is public and can miss what is not. The data comes from other services, which can be late, incomplete or wrong. Paper results say nothing about what real trading would return: real markets have costs, delays and risks the simulation does not model, and leveraged trading can lose more than a simulation suggests. Use what you find here at your own risk.

The service

The site is provided as it is, with no guarantee that it is correct, available or fit for any purpose. It may change, reset its paper desks, or go offline without notice. Do not use it to attack, overload or scrape it or the services it reads from.

Names and marks

Token names, tickers and the stock names behind stock tokens belong to their owners. Listing a token here is not an endorsement of it, and the site is not affiliated with Robinhood, Binance, TradingView or any token's issuer. Charts are drawn with TradingView Lightweight Charts.

What we keep

In your browser only

Your paper desk, its history, your recent tickets and the handle you choose are stored in your browser's own storage. Clearing your browser data removes them. They are not tied to your identity.

What the site's server receives

When you run a ticket: the token's address, the verdict, the score and the short result of each check. When you post to the board of desks: your handle and your paper equity, trade count and reset count. When you ask the conductor: your question and the findings on the page. To limit abuse, the server keeps your IP address next to a count of recent requests for up to an hour, then drops it.

The language model

A question to the conductor, with the findings it refers to, is sent to a language-model provider to be answered (through Surplus, with OpenRouter as the fallback). Do not type personal information into that box.

The second read

For tokens launched on Pons, the ticket also shows a reading by ChargeAI, a separate open model served on Surplus. Only the token's public figures are sent. Its text is another model's opinion: Shuntdesk does not check its figures, and the verdict does not use it.

Services your browser talks to directly

To read prices and chain data, your browser contacts DexScreener, GeckoTerminal, Blockscout, GoPlus, Binance and public Robinhood Chain nodes. Each of them sees your IP address and what you asked for, under its own privacy policy.

What we do not do

No account, no email, no wallet connection, no advertising trackers, no selling of data.
