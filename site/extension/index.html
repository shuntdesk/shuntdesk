The read,
on the token page.

OPEN A TOKEN ON PONS OR DEXSCREENER AND SHUNTDESK'S READ SITS IN THE CORNER: THE CONTRACT, THE LAUNCHER, THE CURVE. IT READS ONLY. IT NEVER ASKS FOR A WALLET OR A SIGNATURE.

InstallChrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers
  1. Download the extension and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (in Edge, edge://extensions).
  3. Turn on Developer mode.
  4. Press Load unpacked and pick the unzipped shuntdesk-extension folder.
  5. Open any token on Pons or DexScreener's Robinhood Chain pages.
What it showsthe same reads as the site
  • The verdict on file, holders, liquidity and the curve
  • What the contract can do: mint, pause, block wallets, change the tax, swap the code
  • What the launcher did with its tokens: sold, sent away, burned
  • What a 0.05 ETH round trip on the curve comes back as
  • A part that could not be read is said as not read
