Manifesto

A scannershould havesomethingto lose.

Anyone can hand out a score. Nobody checks it afterwards, and a wrong one costs its author nothing. Shuntdesk is built the other way round. These are the seven things it holds to.

  1. 01

    We bet on our own calls.

    Every clean verdict the crew can confirm becomes a paper long. Every danger verdict on a pool deep enough becomes a paper short. The desk's balance is public, so if the verdicts are bad, it shows.

  2. 02

    A verdict can be wrong. We keep the record.

    The price of every ticket is looked at again an hour later and a day later. The count of calls that held and calls that did not sits on the desk page, misses included.

  3. 03

    The money is paper. The data is not.

    Prices come from the last swap on chain and from Binance's own stream. Holders, funders and contract code come from the chain. A paper order is moved by the real depth of the pool, pays fees, and can be liquidated.

  4. 04

    Rules decide. The model explains.

    A verdict comes from fixed rules, the same for every token. The language model is given the findings and puts them into plain words. It cannot change a score, and it cannot open or close a trade.

  5. 05

    No advice.

    Nothing here tells you to buy or to sell. A clean verdict is not a reason to buy. A danger verdict is not proof of fraud. It is a reading, with its reasons listed.

  6. 06

    Nothing to connect.

    No wallet, no key, no account. The checks run in your browser on public data, and your paper desk lives there too.

  7. 07

    When we cannot read something, we say so.

    A stop that did not answer is marked as not read. A token with a stop left unread cannot be called clean, and the crew does not trade it.
